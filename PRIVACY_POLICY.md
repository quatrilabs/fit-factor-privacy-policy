# Privacy Policy for FitFactor

*Last Updated: May 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how **FitFactor** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by **Quatri
Labs**, collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application ("App").
We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our
platform.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Usage

### A. No Account Required

FitFactor does not require user account registration, sign-in, or creation of a user profile. You
can use all core logging features of the App without providing personal identity information such as
your name, email address, or phone number.

### B. Fitness & Workout Data (Stored 100% Locally)

All personal fitness data you record in FitFactor—including logged exercises, sets, reps, weights,
workout durations, body weight history, fitness goals, and personal notes—is stored **exclusively on
your device's local memory**.

- We do not upload, transfer, or backup your workout history to any external servers or cloud
  databases managed by us.
- Your personal fitness records remain under your direct control at all times.

### C. Automated Diagnostics & Crash Reporting

To maintain application performance, fix crashes, and improve overall user experience, FitFactor
integrates Google Firebase services:

- **Firebase Crashlytics:** Automatically captures stack traces and anonymized crash reports when an
  unexpected error occurs to help us identify and fix stability issues.
- **Firebase Analytics (if enabled):** Collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic
  information (such as feature usage frequency and basic device characteristics like OS version and
  device model).

This diagnostic data is aggregated, anonymized, and does not contain any of your personal workout
logs, body weight metrics, or identifiable personal details.

## 3. Permissions Used by the App

FitFactor is designed to operate with minimal permissions. Depending on the features enabled:

- **Notifications:** Required only if you choose to enable workout reminders, timer alerts, or rest
  notifications.

---

## 4. Data Control & Deletion

Because FitFactor does not maintain cloud user accounts or external database servers, no online
account deletion request is necessary. You have full control over your data directly on your device:

- **Clearing Data:** You can clear or reset your workout data anytime by using the device settings
  or clearing application data in Android settings
  (`Settings > Apps > FitFactor > Storage > Clear Data`).
- **Uninstalling the App:** Uninstalling FitFactor permanently removes all locally stored workout
  records and preferences from your device.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

FitFactor does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of

13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can
    take appropriate measures.

---

## 6. Security

We value your trust in using FitFactor. Since your workout data remains on your local device, it is
secured by your Android operating system's built-in application sandboxing and device lock
mechanisms. All diagnostic communications with Firebase are encrypted in transit using
industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
